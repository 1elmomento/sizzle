# Captions and the post caption

Two different things.

## Burned-in captions

`caption = true` on a scene burns its line in, word by word, synced to the narration.
Most people watch with the sound off, so every scene that carries information should
have them. Leave them off where the picture already says it — a title card, the reveal,
a closing promise that is already on screen as type.

They sit at 75% of the frame height by default: clear of Instagram's own UI, clear of
the app card above.

## The post caption

**Do not restate the narration.** Someone reading the caption has just heard it. Give
them what the video could not:

- a hook in the first line (it is all that shows before "more")
- *why* the thing exists — the itch it was built to scratch
- a short feature list, worded differently from the video
- what to do next, if there is anything

Keep claims exactly as true as the narration's. If a feature is half-built, it does not
appear in either.
